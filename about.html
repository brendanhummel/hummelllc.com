About

Founded by Brendan Hummel: a senior technologist who has spent 15+ years running real fleets — networks, security, virtualization (VMware and Hyper-V), and vendor stacks — and now works for small businesses that can’t afford a full-time CIO.

Why Hummel LLC exists

Small businesses end up in a familiar spot: a vendor keeps the lights on, a part-time tech fixes what breaks, but nobody owns the whole picture — growth, security, budget, and vendors together. Full-time CIOs are out of reach. Hummel LLC is the middle path: senior IT leadership, honestly scoped, for a fraction of a full-time salary.

What you’re actually getting

Founder-led and senior-level

Decisions come from someone with 15+ years running real fleets — not a ticket queue and not a junior tech.

Compliance-aware by habit

Proven PCI environment experience, and an understanding of the audit and insurance conversations healthcare, legal, finance, and dental clients face — where security reviews actually matter.

Small by design

Few clients, on purpose. No cookie-cutter stacks — and you talk to the person doing the work.

Advice with technical teeth

The vCIO role is backed by deep, current, hands-on capability — the strongest engine is the technical work itself.

Compliance, stated honestly

PCI environment experience is proven and stated plainly. Familiarity with HIPAA, CJIS, and NIST is transferable awareness and context — not a certification claim, and not a promise of audit-level qualification. No more, no less.
