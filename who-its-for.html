Who it’s for

General small businesses, roughly 10–25 employees, with no in-house IT person — any industry, anywhere.

This fits if…

  • You have an IT vendor or a part-time tech — but nobody who owns the whole picture: growth, security, budget, and vendors together.
  • A security scare, a failed audit, or an insurance renewal made you realize nobody is really watching the door.
  • Growth is pulling at the seams — new hires, new locations, new tools — and the technology side is reactive instead of planned.
  • You suspect you’re overpaying a vendor and can’t get a straight answer about what you’re actually getting.
  • You want one senior person who understands the business side and answers your questions in plain English — no jargon, no ticket queue.

Remote-first, anywhere

Meetings, reviews, and oversight are inherently remote-friendly. Columbia, SC roots stay as credibility — and as the option for on-site visits when they genuinely help.

Not the right fit

To be straight about it, we’re not for:

  • Enterprises that need a full IT department.
  • Startups needing a full build-out of infrastructure from zero.
  • One-person shops with very little IT surface — where a retainered leader would be overkill.
  • Anyone needing always-on, 24/7 monitoring and a help desk in year one.

If you’re not sure which side you’re on, ask. A straight answer about fit is part of the service — start there.
