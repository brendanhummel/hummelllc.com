What happens next

  • You describe your situation — size, vendors, what keeps you up at night.
  • We have a straight conversation about fit, scope, and capacity. If we’re not the right fit, we say so.
  • If there’s a match, you get a clearly scoped proposal — what’s included, what it costs, and what it doesn’t cover.

Year-one capacity is about 5 hours a week, so engagements are few on purpose. That’s how you get the person who does the work — not a handoff.
