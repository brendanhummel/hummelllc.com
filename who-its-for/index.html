Who it’s for

General small businesses — roughly 10–25 people, no in-house IT person. Any industry.

You might be a fit if

This sounds like you

  • You have an IT vendor or a part-time tech, but nobody owns the whole picture — growth, security, budget, and vendors together.
  • A security or insurance scare, a failed audit, or growth pains (new hires, new locations) got you paying attention.
  • You suspect you’re overpaying with no visibility into what it buys.
  • You want a senior opinion before the next big IT spend — hardware, software, contract.

Probably not a fit if

  • You’re an enterprise with an IT department.
  • You’re a startup that needs a full build-out.
  • You’re a one-person shop with little IT surface.

Geography

Why this gap is normal

About two-thirds of small businesses have no one who owns security (industry research compiled 2025–26 — directional context). That gap is normal — and it’s exactly the hole this practice exists to fill.
