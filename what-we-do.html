What we do

Senior IT leadership with deep technical substance behind every engagement. The advice is grounded in hands-on experience, not consulting slideware.

The offer

Fractional IT leadership (vCIO) retainers

Quarterly and periodic planning, a technology roadmap, budget guidance, and vendor oversight. The client gets a strategic IT brain on demand.

Network and security assessments

A clear-eyed look at your infrastructure and security posture, with straight talk about risk and cost — security as business protection, not fear.

Backup and disaster-recovery planning

Plans that match how your business actually runs, so recovery is real when it matters. No template theater.

Vendor stack reviews

An independent read on whether you’re getting value from your current IT vendors, services, and subscriptions — and where the waste is.

IT policies and procedures

Policies, onboarding and offboarding procedures, and incident-response guidance built for a business your size — practical, not shelfware.

Scoped hands-on work

Technical work is part of a retainer or quoted per project. Transparently scoped, quoted up front, and done by the person you talk to.

What we are not

Not an always-on, ticket-driven MSP in year one. That is an honest scope decision (capacity around 5 hours a week), not a marketing dodge. If you need 24/7 monitoring and a help desk, Hummel LLC is not the right fit today — and we’ll say so plainly.

Pricing reality

vCIO retainers commonly run $500–3,000/month per client (labeled estimate from public benchmarks, Sept 2026). At roughly 5 hours a week, the honest year-one ceiling is about 2–4 retainer clients — or one retainer plus scoped projects. Every engagement is scoped and quoted up front.

Numbers shown are drawn from public benchmark data and labeled as such. Hummel LLC does not publish its own pricing until an engagement is scoped.
