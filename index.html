What we do

Senior leadership with technical teeth

Fractional IT leadership (vCIO) retainers

Quarterly and periodic planning, a technology roadmap, budget guidance, and vendor oversight. A strategic IT brain on demand — without a full-time hire.

Deep technical substance

Network and security assessments, backup and disaster-recovery planning, vendor stack reviews, IT policies, onboarding and offboarding procedures, incident-response guidance — grounded in hands-on experience, not consulting slideware.
